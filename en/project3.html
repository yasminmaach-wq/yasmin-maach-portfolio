E-Commerce Platform · Product Design · 2024

Products given room to shine.

A calm digital sales platform that presents sustainable products beautifully and makes decisions easier.

Less distraction. More product.

The existing product presentation lacked a clear structure. Sustainable products needed to be showcased at a premium level while simplifying the journey from discovery to purchase.

The new platform combines calm visual presentation with a clear information hierarchy. Product, price and key benefits remain easy to understand at every step.

The process

From discovery to decision.

  1. Analyse product structure and user needs
  2. Define a clear shop and category logic
  3. Design modern, focused product detail pages
  4. Optimise product information and purchase decisions
  5. Refine the experience for desktop and mobile

The outcome

Presented beautifully. Purchased easily.

A modern sales platform that puts sustainable products centre stage and guides users clearly and confidently through the entire purchase journey.
