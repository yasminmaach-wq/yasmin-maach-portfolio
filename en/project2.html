Running Testival · Web Design · 2025

An event that starts online.

A responsive website that communicates energy, organises information and guides visitors directly toward participation.

Complex content. One clear route.

Event information, the agenda, routes and registration needed to be understood quickly. The challenge was to prioritise a large amount of content without losing the event's emotional energy.

The new structure guides visitors intuitively through the experience. Clear hierarchies, strong calls to action and athletic imagery create orientation on every device.

The process

From first click to starting line.

  1. Analyse key event information and user goals
  2. Structure the agenda, routes and registration clearly
  3. Develop responsive wireframes for desktop, tablet and mobile
  4. Create a clear and athletic visual language
  5. Optimise calls to action for direct registration

The outcome

Easier to grasp. Closer to the experience.

The responsive website presents the Running Testival professionally, makes the agenda easy to access and puts registration in focus exactly where it matters.
