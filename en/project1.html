Sport Runclub Branding · 2026

A community in motion.

A clear digital brand presence for a running club that brings people, movement and community together.

From running together to showing up together.

The running club lacked a consistent digital brand presence. Content, community elements and visual communication needed to be brought together more clearly.

The new design creates orientation, feels athletic and professional, and gives the community a recognisable face across every digital touchpoint.

The process

Clear at every step.

  1. Analyse the audience, community and existing brand impact
  2. Define tone of voice, imagery and visual direction
  3. Develop a consistent digital brand presence
  4. Design key platform and social media elements
  5. Refine the system for an athletic and trustworthy impression

The outcome

Dynamic in expression. Consistent in appearance.

A flexible digital identity that presents content and offers clearly and makes the running club visible as a strong, active community.
